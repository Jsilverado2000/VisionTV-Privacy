VisionTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

VisionTV is an IPTV player that lets users load and play compatible playlists and streaming sources that they provide. VisionTV does not provide, sell, or distribute television channels or streaming content.

Information VisionTV Stores

VisionTV is designed to store certain app settings locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • M3U playlist and EPG/XMLTV URLs that you enter.
  • Xtream server address, username, and password that you enter.
  • Your favorite channels.
  • Your recently watched channels.
  • Other preferences required for the app to operate.

These items are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. VisionTV does not operate a user account system for these features and does not intentionally send this locally stored information to VisionTV servers.

Streaming and Third-Party Services

When you load a playlist, EPG source, or streaming URL, VisionTV connects to the internet addresses you provide so that the requested content can be retrieved and played. Those third-party services may receive information such as your IP address and normal connection information as part of providing their service. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

If you use an Xtream-compatible service, the credentials you enter are used by the app to request content from that service. VisionTV does not control how that third-party service processes information.

Analytics and Advertising

VisionTV does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising purposes and does not include advertising in the app. VisionTV does not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.

Tracking

VisionTV does not intentionally track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising or data-broker purposes.

Children's Privacy

VisionTV is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through VisionTV.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by VisionTV remains on your device until you change or remove it, clear the app's stored data, or uninstall the app. You can remove saved information through the app where controls are provided or by removing the app and its local data from your device.

Security

VisionTV is designed to keep the settings it stores locally on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and users should only enter credentials for streaming services they trust.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VisionTV's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning VisionTV, please use the support contact provided on the VisionTV App Store listing.

User-Provided Content

VisionTV is a media player. Users are responsible for ensuring that the playlists, channels, streams, and other content they access through the app are lawful and that they have the necessary rights or permissions to access that content.
